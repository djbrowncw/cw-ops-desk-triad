How the math works
Customer Price = Vendor Cost ÷ (1 - Margin)   $1,000 ÷ 0.60 = $1,666.67 at 40%
Vendor Cost = Customer Price × (1 - Margin)   $1,666.67 × 0.60 = $1,000.00
Margin = (Price - Cost) ÷ Price   ($1,666.67 - $1,000) ÷ $1,666.67 = 40%
Margin is figured on the customer price, not on cost. A 40% margin is a 66.7% markup.
